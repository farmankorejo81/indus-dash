Ready to Dash?

Slide between lanes, avoid traffic and collect coins. Every level gets faster and the road changes its neon mood.

🎮 How to play

Keyboard: ← / → or A / D. Use Space to start or replay.
Touch: Tap the left/right buttons or tap the left/right side of the road.
Goal: Avoid cars, collect gold coins and reach new levels. Your best score is saved on this device.

🌟 Road tips

• Each 250 points advances a level.
• Coins add bonus points.
• Traffic becomes quicker as you progress.
• Sound effects can be switched off anytime.
