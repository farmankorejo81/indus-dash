About Indus Dash

Indus Dash is a free, Pakistan-inspired arcade traffic-dodging game that runs in your web browser.

How to play

  • Use the left and right arrow keys or A and D to switch lanes.
  • On touch screens, use the on-screen buttons or tap the side of the road.
  • Avoid traffic, collect coins, advance through levels and try to beat your best score.
  • Use the sound button to turn game audio on or off.

Our goal

Our goal is to offer a simple, accessible browser game that is quick to start and fun to replay. No account or payment is needed to play the current version.

Contact and feedback

Suggestions and bug reports can be shared through the Indus Dash project on GitHub.
