Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Indus Dash may use when you visit and play the game.

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Information we collect

Indus Dash is a browser game and does not require you to create an account. The game currently saves your best score and sound preference in your browser's local storage on your own device. We do not use that local game data to identify you.

Our hosting provider, Vercel, may process technical information such as request data and security logs to deliver and protect the website. Please refer to Vercel's own privacy information for details about its processing.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

The game itself uses browser local storage for gameplay preferences and scores. If analytics or advertising services are added in the future, those services may use cookies or similar technologies as described in their own policies.

If Google AdSense or other third-party advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve or measure ads and personalize advertising where permitted. You can review Google's advertising and privacy settings to manage available choices.

Children's privacy

Indus Dash is a general-audience game and is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe personal information has been submitted to us, please contact us through the project page linked below.

Third-party services

This website is hosted by Vercel. Links to third-party websites are governed by those websites' own privacy policies. We cannot control the practices of third-party services.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website's features or services change. The date above indicates the latest revision.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the site maintainer through the Indus Dash GitHub repository.
